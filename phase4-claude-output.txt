## H. Known limitations (unresolved, explicitly not silently fixed)
1. **The two "natural pause" eval fixtures still fail**, and now for a clearer reason than before: the gate's design (no duplicate-word-repetition signal, no "glued word" signal) means these specific artifacts pass straight through the fast path uncleaned — `"എനിക്ക്എനിക്ക്"` and `"എനിക്ക് എനിക്ക്"` are never collapsed because neither an unexpected script nor short-fragment pattern fires for them. The quality guard still catches these as `REVIEW` (not `CLEAR`), so nothing is silently marked fine — but the raw duplication artifact does now reach the patient-facing transcript on the fast path where it previously wouldn't have.
2. ASR-level cross-language hallucination (unrelated to this turn's change) remains unmitigated — `languageCodes` is still confirmed unsupported on this API surface, and VAD tuning remains out of scope per your explicit instruction.
